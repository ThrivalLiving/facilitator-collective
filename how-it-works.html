Member experiences

Discover. Experience. Connect.

We aim to offer at least four facilitator-led experiences each month, with no monthly attendance limit. These are strictly for active members, subject to capacity and availability. In-person experiences are prioritised where local chapters exist, with online experiences connecting members further afield. The four-session goal is not a guarantee.

Local chapters

One community. Many places.

Choose a home chapter to discover relevant local facilitators and events. Where available, chapters may have a regular shared venue, while facilitators can also use their own suitable spaces. Chapters are activated as membership grows. All members can access eligible online experiences regardless of location.

Curated introductions

Thoughtful connections, delivered to you.

We aim to introduce you digitally to up to three complementary facilitators each month. You receive a member dashboard notification and email alert, with their profile, why the connection may be valuable and a sample of their work. You decide whether to contact them. These are not mandatory in-person meetings, guaranteed referrals or automatic collaborations.

Your Experience Kit

Show a little of what makes you, you.

Upload one or more examples of your work to your own profile: a short video, PDF, audio, mini-practice or resource link. Fellow facilitators can explore your work before choosing whether to recommend you. Optional member-only discounts or bonuses can also be shared.

The Launchpad

A supportive beginning for new ideas.

Submit a new offering for members to try at no cost, with clear places, dates and expectations. Participating members commit to thoughtful feedback. Public promotion and recommendations are optional and must reflect genuine experience. The Launchpad is designed for testing and learning, not guaranteed exposure.

Everyone contributes

The more we give, the more we grow.

Facilitators contribute their time and gifts through experiences, introductions, feedback, recommendations or community participation. Experience hosts volunteer their time and do not charge participating members. Where a chapter provides a shared venue, eligible facilitators can apply to use it. All submissions are subject to approval.

Founding membership

What your $79 monthly membership includes.

A curated community, member directory, access to available members-only experiences, introductions, samples, Launchpad opportunities, and the chance to collaborate. Individual experience prices, availability, places, discounts and commercial outcomes vary. An illustrative four sessions at $50 each equals $200, but this is not guaranteed membership value.

Your member space

Your profile. Your offerings. Your community.

The planned platform gives approved members their own logins, self-managed profiles and submission forms for experiences, Launchpad opportunities and member offers. Permissions will restrict members to their own content; designated chapter hosts can manage their chapter and administrators oversee approval, bookings and moderation. These are planned capabilities to validate before launch.

About the estimated membership value

How we estimate the value.

The $377 combined monthly figure is a provisional, illustrative value assigned to the mix of member-led sessions, Launchpad access, curated introductions, business support and the member directory/community. It is not a guaranteed saving, verified retail comparison or promise that every benefit is used each month.

The $200 Member Experiences component assumes four sessions at an assigned value of $50 each; session availability, capacity and attendance vary. Other components are estimated using comparable membership-style access and support, not individual retail prices. Business guides are accessible as part of membership rather than newly provided every month. We will review and substantiate these assumptions before a public launch.
